# Apartman Dragićević: accommodation in Bogatić, Mačva, Serbia

Apartman Dragićević is an apartment for up to 4 guests in Bogatić, in the Mačva region of Serbia, at Pavla Orlovića 11. It is 100 m from the town center and 450 m from the bus station. It has one bedroom with two double beds, a living room with a corner sofa that unfolds, and a fully equipped kitchen. Parking and Wi-Fi are free, and guests can use the yard.

Every guest gets clean towels, clean bed linen and a hygiene kit. Coffee and sweets are free.
